Why data is reprocessed

Satellite data does not arrive all at once. A granule for a given day may be delivered hours or days late, and near-real-time streams are superseded by better-calibrated versions. Each sensor therefore has a stability latency: the number of days after which its data for a given day is considered settled.

Within that latency a day's inputs are rebuilt on each run; past it they are left alone. The same logic governs the product: near-real-time output is always rewritten, while a reanalysis product that already exists is never regenerated.

Storage

The raw L2P archive dominates the footprint by a wide margin. The pipeline includes a purge stage that removes downloaded granule directories once they fall outside the rolling window, which is what keeps that number bounded.

Per-sensor windows, latencies, caching rules and the full storage breakdown are in DATA_LIFECYCLE.md.
